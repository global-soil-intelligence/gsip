# Sampling and land-access rules

> **Draft — requires counsel review.** Not in force. The capture app asks contributors to confirm
> these rules before every observation is queued.

## Only where you may dig

- Sample only where you have the permission of the landowner or land manager, or on public land
  whose rules allow it.
- **Never** sample in protected areas (national parks, nature reserves and similar designations),
  in cemeteries or on graves, on archaeological or heritage sites, beside roads or railways, on
  land you know or suspect is contaminated, or where buried pipes or cables may run. Where a
  "call before you dig" service exists (811 in the United States), use it for anything deeper than
  a surface scrape.
- Respect seasonal and wildlife restrictions; do not disturb nesting animals or rare plants.

## How to sample

- Scrape only a small patch, a few centimetres deep: enough for a fresh soil face (shot B).
- Put the soil and any turf back, and take every bit of litter home.
- Clean tools and boots between sites, so you do not spread plant diseases or invasive seeds.

## Stay safe

- Avoid unstable ground, steep banks, water, livestock and extreme weather.
- Children sample only with an adult who has read these rules (see
  [Classroom and minors](CLASSROOM_AND_MINORS.md)).

## Keep photos about the soil

Photograph the soil and the reference card only: no people, faces, vehicle number plates, house
numbers, signs naming a private address, or documents. Photos are published under CC BY-SA 4.0, and
anything in them can be seen by anyone.
