## 2. What GSIP is, and is not

GSIP builds an open map of soil from photographs that people and their agents contribute. Every
estimate GSIP publishes is probabilistic and carries its uncertainty and its basis (image, public
soil maps, nearby laboratory labels). GSIP is **not a laboratory analysis** and gives no
agronomic, engineering, environmental or legal advice. Do not rely on it where an error could cause
harm or loss.

## 3. Your account

**During the invited pilot** (proposed terms version `2026-11-14-v1`): accounts are created by invitation only,
with the e-mail address you gave the organizer. You must be at least **18** years old and live in
the United States, and you confirm your age each time you submit. There are no anonymous accounts
and no classroom contributions in the pilot. Nothing you submit in the pilot is published; see
[the privacy notice](PRIVACY.md#what-becomes-public).

**In the later public service**, which is not open: you may contribute without an e-mail address
(an anonymous account on your device) and add an e-mail address later to use more than one device;
the account stays the same. You must be at least **16** years old, or the age of digital consent
where you live if that is higher, to hold your own account, and you confirm this each time you
submit. Younger people contribute only through a classroom under a teacher of record (see
[Classroom and minors](CLASSROOM_AND_MINORS.md)).

An AI agent may contribute only under the grant of a human of record who has accepted these terms
and is responsible for what the agent submits.

## 4. Your contributions and the licences you grant

You keep ownership of your photographs and observations. When you submit an observation you grant,
for that observation:

- the right to include its structured data in the GSIP database, published under the
  **Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL 1.0)**; and
- the right to publish its photographs, after we remove all embedded metadata and check their
  quality, under **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)**, credited to the
  attribution name you chose or to the default pseudonym "Anonymous soil contributor".

We record this grant for each observation together with the version of these terms and the time
you accepted them, and we never change it afterwards, except that if you delete your account, the
attribution name on it is replaced with the default pseudonym. If these terms change, later
observations are made under a new grant. We will only ever relicense your contributions under licences that are
more open.

You confirm that you took the photographs or have the right to grant these licences, and that they
show no people, faces or private information (see section 6).

**In the invited pilot** you still give this grant with each observation, because the app records
the same grant for every observation. We do not use it to publish. Pilot observations are stored
privately and unverified, are not released, and cannot later be moved into a public release.
Sections 5 to 7 describe publication in the later public service; in the pilot the parts about
releases and public data do not apply, and withdrawal and deletion work as described.

## 5. Withdrawing and deleting

You can withdraw all your contributions, or delete your account, at any time from **Your data** in
the capture app. A withdrawal covers everything you submitted up to that moment, including
observations still waiting to sync on your other devices. It stops publication at once: no release
built after your request contains those observations, and we delete them, with their photographs,
from our private storage within 30 days (normally within the hour). See
[Withdrawal and deletion](WITHDRAWAL_AND_DELETION.md).

Open licences cannot be revoked for copies already released: people who downloaded an earlier
release may keep using those copies under ODbL 1.0 and CC BY-SA 4.0.

## 6. What you may submit

- Follow the [sampling and land-access rules](SAMPLING_AND_LAND_ACCESS.md); you confirm this each
  time you queue an observation.
- Photograph soil and, if you choose, the physical reference card only. No people, faces, vehicle number plates, house
  numbers, documents or anything else that identifies a person or a private address.
- Attribution names must not be another person's name without their consent, impersonate anyone,
  contain insults, links or contact details, or advertise. We review typed names before publishing
  them with photographs and may decline them; until we approve a name, your observation is released
  without its photographs.
- Do not try to identify other contributors, locate their observations more precisely than the
  published cells, overload the service, or work around its limits.

## 7. Privacy

Your precise location is private: public data shows only a broad hexagonal area that at least three
different contributors share. See the [privacy notice](PRIVACY.md).
