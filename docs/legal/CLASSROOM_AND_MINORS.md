# Classroom and minors policy

> **Draft — awaiting product-owner adoption.** Not in force. The age threshold and the consent model are
> **[DECISION]** points.

## Age

- **Independent accounts:** at least **16**, or the age of digital consent where the person lives
  if that is higher **[DECISION]**.
- **Younger people** take part only through a classroom or club under a **teacher of record**.
  They do not create accounts and are never asked for their name, e-mail address or exact location.
- GSIP is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data
  outside a classroom run this way. **[Whether school authorization covers classroom use under COPPA
  and equivalent laws — DECISION.]**

## Teacher of record

The teacher (or club leader) holds the account, accepts the contribution terms for the class's
observations, and:

- obtains any permission the school, parents or guardians require **[DECISION: template consent
  note]**;
- chooses sampling sites under the [sampling and land-access rules](SAMPLING_AND_LAND_ACCESS.md),
  normally school grounds with the school's agreement;
- keeps the default attribution pseudonym, or a class name that does not identify the school's
  location more precisely than the public map does;
- makes sure no student appears in a photograph;
- can withdraw the class's contributions or delete the account at any time from **Your data**.

## Agents and outreach

When GSIP's agent interfaces suggest "Most Wanted" sampling targets (planned for a later work
package; not built yet), they will not route them to minors. GSIP sends no marketing to minors.

## If a child contributed on their own

If we learn that someone under the age limit holds an independent account, we delete the account and
its contributions using the [withdrawal and deletion procedure](WITHDRAWAL_AND_DELETION.md).
