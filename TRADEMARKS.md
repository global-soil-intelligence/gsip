# Trademarks

The GSIP licenses cover the code (MIT), the database and structured data (ODbL 1.0) and the contributed photos (CC BY-SA 4.0). They do not grant rights in the project's names, logos or certification marks. This policy explains how you may use them.

## The marks

Viridis LLC claims trademark rights in:

- **Global Soil Intelligence** and **GSIP**, the project names;
- the GSIP logo and the reference-card design as a source identifier;
- **GSIP Verified**, a certification mark for services and products that meet published GSIP criteria.

These marks are unregistered unless this file says otherwise.

## Always fine

- Saying truthfully that your work uses GSIP: "Built with GSIP data", "Uses the GSIP reference card protocol", "Forked from GSIP".
- Citing the dataset or the project in research, teaching, journalism and documentation.
- Using the names in links to the official repository, site or dataset.
- Printing the unmodified reference card from the official PDF for contributing to GSIP.

## Needs permission

- Naming a product, service, app, company or domain with a GSIP mark, or with a name likely to be confused with one.
- Using the logo, except to link to the official project.
- Running a modified or forked service under the GSIP names. A fork must use a different name and logo, and should say it is based on GSIP.
- Selling merchandise or printed cards that carry the marks.

## GSIP Verified

Only Viridis LLC, or a party it licenses in writing, may award or display **GSIP Verified**. The criteria and the list of licensed parties will be published in this repository before the mark is first used. Using the mark without a license misleads contributors and customers about what has been checked.

## Requests

Ask through a GitHub discussion in the `global-soil-intelligence` organization, or contact the product owner listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Permission is given in writing and can be withdrawn if the use stops meeting this policy.
